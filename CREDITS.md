# Credits

## Game

Nuclear Option and its assets are the property of Shockfront Studios. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Shockfront Studios.

The Hangball Mk.II is based on the stock Eyeball Mk.II. Its in-game bundle uses the stock Eyeball body mesh and a sample of the stock missile body texture, and reuses the game's own materials, shaders, parachute and pylon.
