# Credits

## Game

Nuclear Option and its assets are the property of Shockfront Studios. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Shockfront Studios.

The Hangball Mk.II is a variant of the stock Eyeball Mk.II. Its model and textures are original, made to match the look of the game's AGM-48 family. The bundle ships no game assets; at load time Blueprinter points it at the game's own shaders, effects, sounds, parachute and pylon.
